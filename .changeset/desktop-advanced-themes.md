Add advanced themes that restyle the whole desktop app.

- Liquid Glass: floating frosted-glass panes over a vivid wallpaper tinted by the current cover.
- Ambient: dark cards over a glow taken from the song that is playing.
- Editorial: warm paper, ink rules and serif type.
- Neon Drive: a synthwave horizon with a rolling neon grid.
- Upload your own background image for advanced themes and choose how far it fades.
